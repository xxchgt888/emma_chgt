Biography

My installations, writings and performances explore the concept of the bodies, objects and technologies’s action power, and my work revolves around the duality between organic and digital materials. By shaping ceramic pieces, I explore systems of objects as alien species, derivative materials, and semiotic devices through the prism of an erotic, interspecies relationship. My installations, sculptures and writings are situated at the intersection of the scientific, cryptographic and technological, and seek to challenge the dominant and categorical systems of representation that oppose the human/animal/object/machine/nature/culture. I subscribe to a logic of potential emancipation of non-reproductive desires in the face of capitalist and techno-biopolitical dynamics.
